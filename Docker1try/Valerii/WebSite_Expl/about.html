ABOUT MR.RIMS

Good wheels. A look that feels like you.

Mr.Rims is your place for rims and tire services. Explore styles for your car, ask us about repairing your rims, or get new tires fitted to refresh your setup.

More than just rims

Rims are one of the details that can change the whole character of a car. Some drivers prefer a clean, understated design; others want a bold finish that catches the eye. Our collection brings different looks together in one place.

We can also help with rim repairs and new tires. Whether your rims need attention or it is time for a fresh set of tires, contact us to discuss the service your car needs. Rim and tire sizes must suit your vehicle, so check its specifications or ask us if you are unsure.
